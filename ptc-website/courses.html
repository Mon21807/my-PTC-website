DELPAP Program (4 Phases)

Development Education Leadership and Peace Awareness Program - A progressive 4-phase journey

1

Phase 1: Personal Growth

Self-awareness and personal development foundations. Building emotional intelligence and self-management skills.

  • Self-awareness assessment
  • Emotional intelligence
  • Personal values clarification
  • Goal setting and achievement
Duration: 6 weeks
2

Phase 2: Interpersonal Relationship

Building relationships and community development skills. Effective communication and conflict resolution at personal level.

  • Effective communication
  • Active listening skills
  • Conflict resolution
  • Building trust and rapport
Duration: 8 weeks
3

Phase 3: Social Justice & Analysis

Critical analysis of social justice issues. Understanding systemic injustice and advocacy strategies.

  • Social justice principles
  • Power analysis
  • Advocacy and campaigning
  • Community organizing
Duration: 10 weeks
4

Phase 4: Global Perspective

Understanding social justice from a global viewpoint. International peacebuilding and cross-cultural understanding.

  • Global citizenship
  • International peacebuilding
  • Cross-cultural communication
  • Sustainable development goals
Duration: 12 weeks

Training Methodology

Our interactive and participatory approach to learning

Participatory Learning

Interactive sessions where participants actively engage in discussions, role-plays, and group activities.

Experiential Approach

Learning through experience, reflection, and application to real-world peacebuilding scenarios.
