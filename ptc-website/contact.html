How to Find Us

From Jos city centre, take the Rukuba Road towards Anglo Jos. Look for ECWA Goodnews Church. Our centre is located adjacent to the church, opposite NKST Church, Channel 7 area.

Public Transport: Take buses heading to Anglo Jos
Parking: Available within the premises
