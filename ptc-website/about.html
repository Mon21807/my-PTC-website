Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that drive our commitment to peace building

Our Vision

To become a premier centre of excellence in peace building, fostering a culture of peace and understanding across Nigeria and beyond. We envision a society where conflicts are resolved through dialogue, justice prevails, and communities thrive in harmony.

Our Mission

To empower individuals, communities, and organizations with the knowledge, skills, and attitudes necessary for effective peace building, conflict transformation, and sustainable development through innovative training, research, and advocacy.

Identity

A capacity development centre for peace, justice, leadership and community engagement.

Main Objective

To build capacity in conflict transformation, peace building, humanitarian response, and social protection to increase competent peace building practitioners.

Partnerships

Supported by Mennonite Central Committee (MCC) and part of AWAAN and PeTCAAN networks.

Our Journey

Milestones in our journey of building peace

2016

Establishment

Founded by members of the African Peace Institute and West African Peace Institute Alumni Association of Nigeria (AWAAN) to address growing need for peace building capacity in Nigeria.

2017

First Training Programs

Launched inaugural peace building training programs focusing on conflict transformation and community dialogue in Plateau State.

2018

Partnership with MCC

Established formal partnership with Mennonite Central Committee (MCC) to expand training reach and resources.

2019

Official Registration

Registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of Nigeria, formalizing our legal status as a non-profit organization.

2020

Digital Transition

Adapted training programs for online delivery during COVID-19, expanding reach to participants across Nigeria and West Africa.

2022

Regional Expansion

Expanded programs to Northeast and Northcentral Nigeria, establishing partnerships with local organizations and government agencies.

2023

500+ Trained

Celebrated milestone of training over 500 peace building practitioners from diverse backgrounds and sectors.

2024

New Initiatives

Launched specialized programs in climate change and conflict, gender mainstreaming in peace building, and youth leadership development.
